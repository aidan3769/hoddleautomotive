Auto Maintenance FAQs

Straight answers to the questions we're asked most often.

Brake pad life depends on driving style, load, and the type of pads. Many cars see 30,000–70,000 km between changes, but heavy city driving can wear them sooner. Check your owner's manual for guidance and watch for signs like squealing, longer stopping distances, or a soft pedal. If in doubt, book a brake inspection.

Most batteries last 3–5 years, but heat, short trips, and accessories can shorten that. If you notice slow cranking, dim lights at start-up, or a battery warning light, get it tested.
